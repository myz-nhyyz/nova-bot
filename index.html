AI Chatbot

Talk to an AI that remembers your conversation history, supports custom personas, and works in your preferred language.

  • !chat <msg>Talk to the AI (history per person)
  • !clearchatClear your own conversation history
  • !setaiTurn channel into AI channel
  • !setsharedhistoryWhole channel shares one AI history
  • !persona <style>Set your own AI speaking style
  • !mypersonaView your current persona
  • !resetpersonaReset to default persona
  • !language vi|enSet your bot interface language
⚔️

War Ping / Backup Ping

Coordinate clan wars with a panel-based help desk. Click WAR or BACKUP, fill in the form — the bot handles the rest.

  • WAR buttonPings War role, opens a dedicated thread
  • BACKUP buttonPings Backup role, opens a thread
  • Join / Win / Lose / EndTrack war status in real time
  • Hacker showed upAlert the team mid-war
  • /trust add|remove|listManage trusted members (Manager)
  • /callhacker show|hideToggle Call Hacker button (Manager)
  • /helppanelMove HELP DESK panel (Manager)
  • /config viewView current configuration
🏆

Hero Battlegrounds Event

Run in-server events with a single panel. Members join with one click; managers control the full participant list.

  • Join Event buttonMembers join with one click
  • /event sendPost event panel to the event channel
  • /event testPost a test panel in current channel
  • /event participantsList everyone who joined
  • /event countSee how many people have joined
  • /event removeRemove one person from list
  • /event clearClear the whole participant list
  • /event blacklist add|remove|listBlock a role from joining
🛡️

Auto-Ban Channel

Turn any channel into a ban trap. Anyone who sends a message there gets banned instantly with today's messages purged.

  • /setbanchannel trueEnable auto-ban on this channel (Manager)
  • /setbanchannel falseDisable auto-ban (run in ban channel)
  • /banwhitelist add @userExempt a user from ban (bot owner)
  • /banwhitelist remove @userRemove exemption (bot owner)
